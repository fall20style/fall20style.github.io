## Hang on to와 Steer clear of 가 들어간 문장 해석

1. Make sure to hang on to your receipt in case you want to return the jacket.

2. I try to steer clear of spicy food before an important exam.

3. We need to hang on to our best employees by offering better benefits.

4. It’s best to steer clear of political topics during small talk with clients.

--------------------------------------------
## 1. In Everyday Life (일상생활)

* Hang on to: "Make sure to hang on to your receipt in case you want to return the jacket."
(재킷을 환불하고 싶을 수도 있으니 영수증을 꼭 보관해 두세요(버리지 마세요).)
* Steer clear of: "I try to steer clear of spicy food before an important exam."
(중요한 시험 전에는 매운 음식을 피하려고 노력해요.)

## 2. At Work / Professional (직장 및 비즈니스)

* Hang on to: "We need to hang on to our best employees by offering better benefits."
(더 나은 복지를 제공해서 우리의 유능한 직원들을 어떻게든 붙잡아야 합니다.)
* Steer clear of: "It’s best to steer clear of political topics during small talk with clients."
(고객과의 가벼운 대화에서는 정치적인 주제를 피하는 것이 가장 좋습니다.)

## 3. Financial / Investment (재테크 및 자산)

* Hang on to: "Even when the stock market drops, experienced investors often hang on to their shares."
(주식 시장이 폭락할 때도 경험 많은 투자자들은 종종 주식을 팔지 않고 계속 보유합니다.)
* Steer clear of: "Financial experts advise beginners to steer clear of high-risk investments."
(금융 전문가들은 초보자들에게 고위험 투자를 멀리하라고(피하라고) 조언합니다.)
